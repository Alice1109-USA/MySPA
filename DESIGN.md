# Signature SPA Design System

## Direction

Editorial luxury with recognizable La Belle purple heritage. The visual system is composed, private, and image-led: deep royal aubergine creates atmosphere; warm cream carries long-form reading; antique gold is reserved for actions and precise details.

## Color

- Aubergine 950 `#17091D`: deepest backgrounds and footer.
- Aubergine 900 `#24102D`: primary dark surface.
- Aubergine 800 `#381744`: service and CTA surfaces.
- Aubergine 700 `#542462`: interactive emphasis.
- Muted purple `#80558A`: secondary labels on light surfaces.
- Lilac `#D8C6DD`: facial and care sections.
- Warm cream `#F8F3EB`: editorial reading surfaces.
- Paper `#FFFDF9`: service-page body.
- Antique gold `#C7A15C`: actions, rules, focus and brand detail.
- Light gold `#E7CF9C`: text accents on dark surfaces.

Gold is an accent, not a fill color for large areas. Purple remains dominant only in high-impact sections; reading areas stay warm and light.

## Typography

- Display: Italiana, with Georgia as fallback.
- Body and navigation: DM Sans, with Arial as fallback.
- Display headings use restrained negative tracking and generous scale.
- Body copy should remain within a comfortable editorial measure.

## Surfaces and Imagery

- Service imagery is full-bleed or framed with a fine offset gold rule.
- Images use mildly reduced saturation and controlled contrast.
- Do not use images that imply a real Redmond interior until verified photography exists.
- Shadows are broad and soft; borders and shadows are not combined on the same decorative surface unless structurally required.

## Interaction

- Gold focus rings are mandatory for keyboard navigation.
- Hover movement uses transforms, not layout properties.
- Buttons invert between aubergine and antique gold.
- Reduced-motion preferences collapse nonessential transitions.

## Responsive Behavior

- Desktop navigation uses measured three-column alignment.
- At 900px and below, navigation becomes a stacked menu and imagery becomes full-width.
- Service-photo offset frames contract on smaller screens to avoid horizontal overflow.

## Editorial Layout

- Homepage sections use contrasting spatial rhythms rather than repeating the same two-column block.
- The philosophy section uses a wide editorial grid and oversized low-contrast brand lettering.
- Massage services use a sticky introduction beside a generous ruled list.
- The three-image gallery deliberately varies image height on desktop and returns to an even vertical sequence on mobile.
- Facial care uses a sticky narrative column and a larger treatment matrix.
- Head Spa is expressed as a full-width image-and-copy feature.
- Inner pages use a large title field followed by a magazine-like reading column, framed service image, and restrained visit sidebar.

## Content Boundary

Visual styling may change, but service copy, business facts, URL paths, phone number, gift-card link and Redmond SEO content remain product truth and require explicit approval to change.
